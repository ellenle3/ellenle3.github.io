Outreach

I regularly participate in outreach events around the Big Island, but am mainly involved with:

  • Maunakea Scholars: I have advised high school students on writing observing proposals (mainly for planetary observations), executing their programs on IRTF, and interpreting their data.

  • Journey Through The Universe: I visit public schools and teach children about star formation, the Solar System, and optics by designing interactive activities for them.

Mentorship

I have mentored several high school and undergraduate students on longer term projects through a variety of arrangements. You likely have different life experiences, goals, and values than I do, so I try my best to be understanding of that. I am happy to discuss accomodation for a variety of communication and working styles.

(That being said, if you are wondering how I am as a mentor, it is probably best to ask someone who has worked under me in the past…)
