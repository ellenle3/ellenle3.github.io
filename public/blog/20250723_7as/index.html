Seven arcsecond seeing

July 23, 2025

We just came out of an observing run with the IRTF ASM. The weather… wasn’t great… but we were able to obtain useful data. One night Subaru reported 7" seeing.

Our 12x12 Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensor looked like wind blowing through a field of wheat.

For reference, the subapertures are 4.5" across and so the spots usually look like this:

Gusts of terrible seeing are not too uncommon, but days of sustained seeing above 5" is highly unusual on Maunakea. Some conditions are so miserable that they wrap around to being spectacular!
